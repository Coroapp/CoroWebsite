Privacy Policy

Last updated October 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Coro ("Coro," "we," "us," or "our") collects when you use the Coro mobile application and coroconnect.com (the "Service"), how we use and share it, how long we keep it, and the choices you have. It should be read together with our Terms of Service.

In short: we collect what we need to run a private app for small friend groups, and nothing more. We use no analytics SDKs, no advertising identifiers, and no ad networks, and nothing in Coro tracks you across other companies' apps or websites. We do not sell or rent your personal information, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.

3. Device permissions

  • Camera and photo library are used only when you choose to take or select a photo to share or to set as your profile picture.
  • Contacts are read on your device only when you choose to invite someone, so you can pick who to invite. Your contact list is never uploaded to our servers and we never store it.
  • Notifications are used only to deliver Coro notifications you have allowed.

Each permission is optional, and you can change it at any time in your device settings. Coro does not collect your precise or approximate location.

4. How we use information

We use the information described above to: provide, maintain, and improve the Service; create and secure your account; display your content to the members of your Circles; send notifications you have enabled; process and verify Coro Plus subscriptions; respond to your requests; review reports, investigate violations of our Terms (including misuse of the Service as a storage service), and keep the Service and its users safe; and comply with legal obligations. We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not use your content to train artificial-intelligence models.

5. Who can see your information

Other users. Content you share is visible only to the members of the Circle you share it with. Other members can see your display name, profile photo, and profile color; your phone number, email address, date of birth, and moderation records are never shown to other users. Coro has no public profiles, no search, and no discovery feed.

Our moderators. When content or a profile is reported, authorized Coro moderators may view the reported content and the information reasonably necessary to review it, even if they are not members of that Circle. Moderators do not otherwise have access to Circles' content.

7. How long we keep information

  • Posts are deleted automatically 24 hours after they are shared, unless a member has saved them; a saved post is kept until the last member who saved it removes it.
  • Scrapbook photos can be added for a limited window after a Hangout and then remain part of the Circle's record until they are deleted, the Circle is deleted, or the uploader's account is deleted.
  • Invite links expire 30 minutes after they are created. Photo download links are signed and expire automatically.
  • Unused files, such as photos from deleted posts or Circles, are removed from storage automatically.
  • Account data is kept until you delete your account, as described below.
  • Reports and moderation records may be kept for as long as needed to enforce our Terms and prevent banned users from returning, and as required by law.
  • Backups and logs maintained by our infrastructure providers are retained for limited periods before being overwritten.

8. Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time from Settings → Delete account. Deletion is immediate: your profile, posts, scrapbook photos, saved posts, and push tokens are removed, you leave all of your Circles, and any Sign in with Apple authorization is revoked. Hangouts, ideas, and traditions you created that belong to a Circle remain for the Circle's other members without your name attached. If you cannot sign in, see Delete your account. Deleting your account does not cancel a Coro Plus subscription; cancel it in your App Store or Google Play settings.

9. Your rights and choices

You can view and edit your display name and photo in Settings, turn notifications off, block other users, and delete your content and account at any time. Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to request access to, correction of, a copy of, or deletion of your personal information, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent. To make a request, email help@coroconnect.com; we may need to verify your identity first. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights. If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, we process your information as needed to perform our contract with you (providing the Service), for our legitimate interests in keeping the Service safe and preventing abuse, and to comply with law, and you may lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

10. Security

We protect your information with measures including encryption in transit, access controls that limit each user to the Circles they belong to, and signed, expiring links for photos. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, however, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. International transfers

Coro is operated from the United States, and your information may be stored and processed in the United States and in other countries where our service providers operate, which may have data protection laws different from those where you live.

12. Children

Coro is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. We ask for a date of birth at sign-up, and an account that does not meet the minimum age is deleted. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with information, contact us and we will delete it.

13. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date above, and for material changes we will provide additional notice, such as in the app.

14. Contact

Questions about this Policy or your information: help@coroconnect.com.
