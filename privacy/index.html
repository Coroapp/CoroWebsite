Privacy Policy

Last updated September 15, 2026

Coro is a private app for small friend groups. This page explains what we collect, why, how long we keep it, and how you can delete it. It applies to the Coro mobile app and to coroconnect.com.

What we collect

DataWhy
Phone numberSigning in and verifying your account
Name and date of birthYour profile, and confirming you're 13 or older
Profile photoShown to the people in your circles
Photos you post or add to a scrapbookShown to the members of that circle
Text you write — hangout details, notes, captions, ideas, traditionsThe core of how Coro works

Contacts are read from your device only when you choose to send an invite, so we can help you find who to invite. Your contact list is never uploaded to our servers and we never store it. You can skip this permission entirely and still use Coro.

What we don't do: no analytics SDKs, no advertising identifiers, no ad networks, and nothing that tracks you across other apps or websites. We don't sell your data or share it with advertisers, ever.

Who can see it

Everything you post is scoped to the specific Circle you shared it with — the small group of friends who invited you or whom you invited. Coro has no public profiles, no search, and no discovery feed. Nobody outside your circles can see your content.

How long we keep it

  • Posts disappear automatically after 24 hours unless someone bookmarks them.
  • Scrapbook photos can be added for 48 hours after a hangout, then that window closes; the entry itself stays as part of your circle's permanent record until a member deletes it.
  • Signed download links for photos expire after 24 hours (a new one is generated automatically whenever the app needs it).
  • Account data is kept until you delete your account, at which point it's removed as described below.

Who we share it with

We use a small number of infrastructure providers to run Coro — currently Supabase (database, authentication, file storage) and Twilio (sending verification codes by SMS). They process data on our behalf under their own security commitments and don't use it for their own purposes. We don't share your data with advertisers, data brokers, or anyone else.

Your controls

You can block or report anyone in your circles directly in the app. Reports are reviewed by us within 24 hours. You can delete your account at any time from Settings → Delete Account — this is immediate and removes your profile, posts, and photos from our systems. See Delete your account for the email option if you can't sign in.

Children

Coro is not for anyone under 13. We ask for a birthday at signup, and an account that fails that check is deleted automatically before it's ever used.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: help@coroconnect.com.
