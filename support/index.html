Support

Last updated September 15, 2026

Something not working, or something you'd rather ask a person about? Email help@coroconnect.com — a person reads every message, and we typically reply within a couple of days. Reports about abusive content or behavior get a response within 24 hours.

What is Coro?

A private app for small friend groups. You propose hangouts, see who's free, vote on plans, and keep a shared scrapbook — all scoped to a small "Circle" of people who know each other.

How do I get in? I don't have an invite.

Coro is invite-only. Ask a friend who already uses it to send you an invite link from inside the app — there's no public signup or search.

I didn't get my verification code

Double-check the phone number and signal, then try "Resend code." If it still doesn't arrive after a couple of tries, email us the phone number you're signing up with and we'll look into delivery on our end.

How do I report or block someone?

Long-press a post, photo, or person and choose Report or Block. Blocking hides their content from you immediately. Reports go to us and get a response within 24 hours. Manage blocked accounts anytime in Settings → Blocked accounts.

How do I delete my account?

Settings → Delete Account inside the app. See Delete your account for details, including what to do if you can't sign in.

Is Coro free?

Yes — no subscription, no in-app purchases.
