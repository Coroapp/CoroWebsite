Delete your account

Last updated September 15, 2026

You're always free to leave. Here's what deleting your account does and the two ways to do it.

What gets deleted

Deleting your account removes your profile, your posts, your scrapbook photos, and your avatar from our systems, and signs you out everywhere. This can't be undone. Content you added to a shared scrapbook that other members also contributed to (like a group photo entry) stays visible to them, minus your own photos.

In the app (immediate)

  1. Open Coro and go to Settings.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account.
  3. Confirm. Your account and data are removed right away.

Without the app

If you can't sign in, email help@coroconnect.com from the address you'd like us to confirm with, and include the phone number on the account. We'll verify it's you and delete the account, typically within a few business days.

Deleting your account is different from leaving a single Circle. To leave just one Circle and keep your account, use Your Circles → Leave inside the app instead.

Questions

help@coroconnect.com — see also our Privacy Policy for how long data is kept before deletion.
