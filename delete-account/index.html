Delete your account

Last updated October 7, 2026

You're always free to leave. Here's what deleting your account does and the two ways to do it.

Paying for Coro Plus? Cancel it first in App Store or Google Play subscriptions. Deleting your account does not cancel the subscription, and Apple or Google will keep billing until you do.

What gets deleted

Deleting your account removes your profile, your posts, your scrapbook photos, your saved posts, and your avatar from our systems, removes you from every Circle, revokes Sign in with Apple if you used it, and signs you out everywhere. This can't be undone. Plans, ideas, and traditions you created stay with the Circle for its other members, without your name on them. A Circle you own passes to its longest-standing member; a Circle you're the last member of is deleted with you.

In the app (immediate)

  1. Open Coro and go to Settings.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account.
  3. Confirm. Your account and data are removed right away.

Without the app

If you can't sign in, email help@coroconnect.com from the address you'd like us to confirm with, and include the phone number on the account (or, if you signed in with Apple or Google, the email address on that account). We'll verify it's you and delete the account, typically within a few business days.

Deleting your account is different from leaving a single Circle. To leave just one Circle and keep your account, use Your Circles → Leave inside the app instead.
